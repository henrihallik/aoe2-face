Name: Mirrorwake
Category: Unconventional 1v1 hybrid
Description: The complete playable island forms an original smiling terrain portrait at CaptureAge full-map zoom. Players begin on large mirrored ear plateaus with the required 9-villager start, guaranteed building room, a dense 180-tile front forest, two additional 64-tile home woodlines, and fixed mirrored food and mine slots. The open face supports direct aggression while harvestable pine hair and salt-and-pepper beard create choppable flank routes. Broad ear coves open onto dockable water terrain, with six shore fish and fourteen deep fish distributed across ten fixed locations per side. Raised cheeks, bright eyes, visible teeth, and an exaggerated upturned mouth preserve the smile at spectator scale. Five relics mark both eyes, both cheeks, and the central raised nose. The fixed 120 x 120 layout is horizontally mirrored, while player colors swap sides independently. No unit or building behavior is modified.
Recommended settings: 1v1, Tiny, Standard resources, Dark Age, Standard or Conquest victory.
